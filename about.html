Visual Storyteller

Dan Redding

I'm a children's book author and illustrator with bachelor's degrees in illustration (Parsons School of Design) and creative writing (The New School University). My work is grounded in a lifelong love of visual storytelling, combining relatable stories with imagination and humor.

My career as an Art Director and designer has spanned more than a decade leading creative work for national brands and organizations. My artwork and illustrations have appeared in Men's Health and Women's Health, and my design writing has been published by W. W. Norton & Company in Back to the Lake and The Norton Sampler. I have also contributed articles to Smashing Magazine.
